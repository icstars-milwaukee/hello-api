# Input: Rough User Stories (unrefined)

These are the raw items as they came off the whiteboard at intake. They are **not** sprint-ready — no roles, no reasons, no acceptance criteria, no estimates, no owners.

| ID | Raw item | Why it isn't ready |
| --- | --- | --- |
| RAW-1 | "Login works" | Not a story. No role, no goal, no reason. "Works" is not testable — works for whom, under what conditions? |
| RAW-2 | "User can reset password" | Has a role and a goal but no reason, no criteria, no scope (email link? security questions? SMS?). |
| RAW-3 | "Fix signup" | A bug report wearing a story costume. What is broken? For which users? |
| RAW-4 | "Dashboard" | A noun, not a behavior. No user, no outcome. |
| RAW-5 | "Email stuff" | Unestimable. Could be one hour or one quarter. |

## Selection for this refinement pass

**RAW-1** and **RAW-2** were selected because they are the two items blocking the authentication epic — nothing else in the backlog can be demoed until a user can get into the product and stay in it. They become `STORY-101` and `STORY-102` in [`refined-backlog.md`](refined-backlog.md).

RAW-3, RAW-4, and RAW-5 stay in the product backlog and are explicitly marked **Not Ready** — see the backlog-health section of the refined backlog.
